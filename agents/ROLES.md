# Roles and authority — Azure Mines

**Status:** Project-specific responsibilities · **Reviewed:** 2026-11-12

## Purpose
Clarify who designs, implements and accepts work without assuming multiple human team members.

## Human instructions
The project owner approves game design decisions, major dependency/toolchain changes, story canon, merges, and final playable-slice acceptance.

## AI agent instructions
State the proposed scope and primary role, report evidence and open questions, and do not claim another agent or human approved changes.

## Assignments
- **Product/world/creative owner:** Human project owner.
- **Architecture/design engineering:** AI-assisted proposal and implementation, subject to project owner review.
- **Combat/QA/technical art:** AI-assisted implementation and automated checks; physical playtesting and visual acceptance need actual recorded evidence.
- **Git merge authority:** Human project owner; no autonomous merge or force-push.
- **Handoff:** Branch and commit, changed paths, test command/results, screenshots when available, risks and NOT RUN checks.

## Approval policy
Document approval in the issue/PR or conversation before significant gameplay rule changes. Trivial fixes can be approved by a bounded owner instruction.
