## Principles worth coming back to

**Places have reasons to exist.** Borrow relationships between terrain, water, work, transport and settlement—not only attractive silhouettes.

**The supernatural has relationships, not only statistics.** Hospitality, fair payment, promises, restitution and local knowledge can matter as much as combat.

**Folklore should reward attention.** Give players discoverable clues and consistent local rules. Do not make every spirit obey one universal weakness or every strange event a lethal trick.

**Preparation becomes narrative.** Food, rope, coins, blankets and pack animals can solve problems beyond replenishing a rest resource. Avoid reducing every offering to a guaranteed buff.

**Keep uncertainty useful.** Local belief, historical evidence, author-only fictional truth and player knowledge are different things. Some legends may be mistaken, some exploited by ordinary criminals and some genuinely supernatural.

## How to use and extend the notebook

Refer to stable identifiers in later discussions: for example, **"develop FOL-05 Bluecap"** or **"combine LOC-04 with FOL-02"**. The explicit anchors `#loc-04` and `#fol-05` remain stable even if a heading changes.

When an adaptation is approved, retain the original research and add a dated decision link. Record the chosen fictional name/location, what is actually true, what inhabitants believe, what the player can learn and any required systems. Update the world bible and the relevant design document separately. Do not silently promote research into canon.

**Canon safeguards:** The established identities of Seabrook, Harrowford, Old Rookhold, Vale Manor and the high-moor tower remain separate. The working deity Lethiel is not replaced by these historical references. The goblin cave's current design and the campaign's undisclosed deeper-mine story remain unchanged. No new characters, gods, factions, map positions or implementation tasks are created by saving this notebook.

## Sources and archival scope

The linked research pages retain the sources cited in the 13 November conversation and label game adaptations separately. This preservation pass did not independently re-audit every source. Important historical details, regional attributions and source availability should be rechecked before publication as factual material. Older folklore collections and modern heritage summaries are not interchangeable with archaeological evidence.

This is a structured record of the ideas, not a verbatim chat export. Referenced photographs and third-party webpage images have not been copied into the repository; no asset reuse rights are asserted. The archive introduces no change to the repository's existing licensing position.
