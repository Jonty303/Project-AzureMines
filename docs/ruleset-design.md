# Classic 5E character rules — v0.1

This is a **house-ruled adaptation** of D&D SRD 5.2.1, not a claim of complete official 2024-rules compatibility.

## Agreed scope

- Playable levels: **1–5** initially.
- Four races: Human, Dwarf, Elf (High Elf fixed lineage for now), Halfling.
- Five fixed-progression classes: Champion, Priest, Thief, Evoker, Hunter.
- Four adventurers in the initial party; retain design headroom for up to six.
- Backgrounds and multiclassing omitted. No selectable subclasses or general-purpose feat selection.
- Ability scores: standard array or 27-point buy, plus flexible **+2/+1 to different scores** in place of background bonuses.
- The normal class chassis and the SRD subclass features combine into one class definition; subclass features arrive at their **original levels**.
- Current fixed choices include Champion **Defense**, Priest **Protector**, Evoker **Arcana Scholar Expertise**, Hunter **Archery** and **Horde Breaker**, Thief **Sleight of Hand** and **Stealth** for initial Expertise.
- Human Origin feat fixed to **Skilled**; other racial traits retained. Race/lineage bonus spells require their own implementation.
- At level 4, the first pass grants **+2 to primary class ability** as an ASI instead of selecting a feat. Revisit score cap and flexibility during balancing.
- Spell preparation remains a player choice, even while advancement branching is removed.

## Core magic targets

- Priest: healing and support (Bless, Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Guiding Bolt), restoration and undead control; Life Domain prepared spells must work at the levels they are granted.
- Evoker: Magic Missile, Fire Bolt, Scorching Ray, Shatter, Fireball; defensive and control magic; Evocation Savant spells go into a spellbook, not automatic preparation.
- Hunter: Hunter's Mark, Ensnaring Strike, Hail of Thorns, Cure Wounds and utility/nature spells.
- More extensive magical exploration and free-form effects follow the core combat milestone.
